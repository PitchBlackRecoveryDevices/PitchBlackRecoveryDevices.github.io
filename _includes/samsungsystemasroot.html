Samsung System-as-Root

This device uses System-as-Root with A-only partitions. Magisk and Recovery share the same partition.

Booting Logic:

  • Power + Vol Up: Boots to Magisk/PBRP (Recovery)
  • Power only: Boots to Stock System (No Root)

Recommendation: Root your device with Magisk first, then pre-root the PBRP image before flashing it to recovery.
